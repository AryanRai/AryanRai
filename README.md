## 👍 **What I'm up to**

- **Imitation Machines - Robotics Systems Engineer, Intern | Mar 2026-present**  
  Integrating the UR10e, Inspire RH56 hand and Elephant Robotics M750 with ROS 2, Isaac Sim, MuJoCo and Gazebo. Building motion control, VR teleoperation and demonstration-data workflows, and training and deploying ACT, Diffusion Policy and VLA policies with PyTorch and LeRobot.
- **[Perception](https://prxptn.com) - Co-Founder, Hardware, Robotics & Embedded | 2026-present**  
  Building Orbit for mission design, orchestration and real-time operations of autonomous drones and robots. Working across ArduPilot/MAVLink, video and telemetry pipelines, Wi-Fi HaLow mesh communications, edge compute and cloud infrastructure. Selected for the Antler AUS16 residency.

## 🛠 **Experience & team projects**

- **Sydney Interplanetary Rover Initiative - Electrical Co-Lead | Mar 2024-Mar 2026**  
  Led a 17-engineer electrical, embedded and controls team through the Australian Rover Challenge 2026. Designed rover power distribution and custom KiCad PCBs, developed Teensy/C++ firmware and CAN-based FOC motor control, and integrated sensing, cameras, Jetson compute and long-range telemetry.
- **ANT61 - Embedded Systems Intern | Jun-Jul 2025**  
  Developed STM32/C++ firmware for Beacon RevG space robotics hardware, including GNSS/UART acquisition, DMA/interrupt-driven data handling, and CubeSat Space Protocol communications with libcsp, KISS framing and CRC32 validation.
- **USYD Rocketry Team - Embedded & Ground Control Engineer | Feb 2024-Jan 2025**  
  Designed schematics and firmware for the team's first in-house STM32 datalogger, with RS485 and LoRa telemetry. Built LabJack-based tank-test instrumentation and custom test software.
- **Sydney UAV Engineering, Project FASTR - Drone Flight Engineer | Feb-Sep 2024**  
  Built and calibrated a PX4/Pixhawk quadrotor with Raspberry Pi, ROS 2 and OpenCV; served as primary pilot for field flight tests.

## 🧰 **Projects & Creations**

- **[HowYouSeeMe](https://github.com/AryanRai/HowYouSeeMe)** - ROS 2 perception that combines RGB-D geometry, object detection, segmentation and face identity into a persistent semantic world state. Exposes that state through MCP, with Rerun visualisation; uses Kinect v2, ORB-SLAM3, YOLO11, SAM2/FastSAM, InsightFace and TSDF.
- **Orbit at [Perception](https://prxptn.com)** - Mission planning, orchestration and fleet operations for drones and robotic systems, with operator-supervised execution, video, telemetry and simulation workflows.
- **[SatMap](https://github.com/AryanRai/SatMap)** - Satellite uptime and communication-opportunity simulation between an ANT61 Beacon-equipped satellite and the Iridium constellation, with 2D/3D visualisation. First place at the USRC × ANT61 Space Handshakes Hackathon.
- **Autonomous Warehouse Robotics - MTRX3760 (2025)** - ROS 2 navigation, mapping and integration on TurtleBot, using LiDAR, cameras and SLAM in Gazebo/RViz for inspection and delivery.
- **[DroidCore](https://github.com/AryanRai/DroidCore)** - A customisable robotics platform.
- **[BlueLily](https://github.com/AryanRai/BlueLily)** - A configurable sensing, control, communications and real-time logic platform for robotics, rocketry, payloads and CubeSats.
- **[Comms](https://github.com/AryanRai/Comms)** - Centralised communications dashboards for control and ground stations.
- **StarSim** - An embedded/robotics physics library for simulation and real-time deployment.
